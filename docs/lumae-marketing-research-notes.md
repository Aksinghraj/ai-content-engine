# Lumae Marketing Research Notes

## Product and positioning evidence

Lumae's public site currently leads with "Your idea. Ready for every channel." It positions the product as a creator-focused system that turns one idea into posts, scripts, captions, formats, saved work, and a publishing plan. Its strongest differentiators are multi-channel continuity, content workflow, free entry, and account/privacy controls.

The public pages currently contain one offer inconsistency to correct in campaign copy: the home page says "Three free generations," while the pricing page presents five free generations per day. The marketing plan should use only the confirmed product entitlement after the product team resolves this mismatch.

## External findings

The IAB's 2025 Creator Economy Ad Spend & Strategy Report says U.S. creator ad spend is projected at $37 billion in 2025, 26% above the prior year. It also reports that 48% of creator ad buyers consider creators a must-buy channel and highlights measurement and operational tools as improvement areas. This supports Lumae's emphasis on a measurable workflow rather than generic AI output. Source: https://www.iab.com/insights/2025-creator-economy-ad-spend-strategy-report/

ProductLed's 2025 benchmark article reports a 12% median visitor-to-freemium conversion rate and an approximately 9% average free-to-paid conversion rate across models. It also reports materially higher conversion for organizations using product-qualified leads and notes that activation is not consistently tracked. These figures are directional benchmarks, not Lumae forecasts; Lumae should measure its own activation events and paid conversion before setting spend targets. Source: https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-benchmarks
