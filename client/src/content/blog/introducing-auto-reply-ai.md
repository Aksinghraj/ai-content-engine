Every social post creates an inbox. A useful comment becomes a question, a question becomes a sales opportunity, and a reply that arrives too late can look like no reply at all. For smaller teams, the real problem is not writing one response. It is maintaining a thoughtful response habit across every conversation.

## The engagement gap

Most brands publish content with a plan, then manage comments in fragments. Someone checks Instagram between meetings. Someone else catches LinkedIn notifications later. The result is uneven: valuable questions wait, simple acknowledgements consume time, and the tone changes depending on who happens to answer.

Auto-Reply AI is designed to make this manageable. It helps you set response rules, identify the intent behind a comment, and prepare contextual replies that sound useful rather than automatic.

## How a contextual reply is created

The workflow begins with the connected account and the rules you choose. Lumae considers the incoming comment, the original post context, and the response style you want for that account. It then creates a suggested reply for the right kind of interaction instead of treating every comment as identical.

You can keep the system in review mode while you learn how it behaves. When you are ready, approved rules can handle routine interactions while escalation conditions route sensitive, negative, or high-intent messages to a person.

## Before and after

Before Auto-Reply AI, a creator may post a product tutorial at 10 a.m., see a pricing question at lunch, and respond after the prospect has already moved on. The response is not bad; it is simply late.

With a focused rule in place, the comment can receive a concise, on-brand acknowledgement and a helpful next step while the conversation is still active. If the question needs a human answer, the workflow can flag it rather than improvising.

## Start with the conversations you already have

Auto-Reply AI is not meant to replace good judgement. It is meant to remove the repetitive work that prevents good judgement from reaching the comments that need it most.

Try Auto-Reply AI from the **Automation** area in Lumae AI, start with a small review workflow, and refine the rules around the conversations that matter to your audience.
